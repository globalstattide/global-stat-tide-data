# Global Stat Tide — Open Data

The research behind every ranking on the **[Global Stat Tide](https://www.youtube.com/@GlobalStatTide)** YouTube channel, published openly so anyone can check our numbers, reuse them, or correct us.

**40 datasets · 4,114 rows · every dataset linked to its video, its sources and its method**

## How we work
- Every video starts as a research spreadsheet built from public, citable sources: IMF, World Bank, UN, Forbes, Britannica, Wikipedia (cited entries), official records and major news outlets.
- Before publishing here, each dataset was **re-checked in November 2026** against the video's on-screen cards and against its sources.
- Where the video and the evidence disagree, the dataset shows **both** and explains the correction. We would rather show our mistakes than hide them.
- Most datasets include a **Confidence** column: *Verified* = two independent sources agree; *Partial* = one source; *Unconfirmed* = sources conflict or none found.
- Each folder contains `data.csv` (browse it directly on GitHub), an Excel version, and a `README.md` describing columns, sources and corrections.
