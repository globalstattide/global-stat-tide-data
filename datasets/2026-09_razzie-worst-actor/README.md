# Worst Actor Ever? 45 Years of Razzie Winners (1981–2026)

**Video:** [Worst Actor Ever? 45 Years of Razzie Winners (1981–2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X7KnAhiwcfY), published 13 September 2026
**Rows:** 46 Golden Raspberry (Razzie) Worst Actor winners, from Neil Diamond (1981) to Ice Cube (2026), in ceremony order. The 1997 award was a tie, so that year has two rows

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ceremony Year, Winner, Film (as shown on card) | As shown in the video |
| Film Year | The year the films were released (the ceremony is held the following year) |
| Winning Film(s), official record | Every film the award was given for. Many winners were named for more than one film; the cards show one |
| Win No. for This Winner | 1 for a first Worst Actor win, 2 for a second, and so on |
| Also an Oscar Winner?, Oscar Detail | Whether the winner has also won an Academy Award: **acting**, **non-acting** (directing, writing, music, producing) or **honorary** |

## By the numbers
- **Most wins:** Sylvester Stallone **4** (1985, 1986, 1989, 1993). Kevin Costner and Adam Sandler **3** each; John Travolta and Pauly Shore **2** each.
- **37 different winners** across the 46 rows. The Wayans brothers (2007) and the Jonas Brothers (2010) shared their awards.
- **Oscar winners:** 8 people, in 10 rows. 4 hold acting Oscars: Laurence Olivier, Roberto Benigni, Jared Leto and Jon Voight. Kevin Costner (3 rows), Prince and Ben Affleck won non-acting Oscars, and Tom Cruise received an honorary Oscar in November 2025.
- **2 US presidents** won for appearing as themselves in documentaries: George W. Bush (2005) and Donald Trump (2019).

## Sources
Golden Raspberry Award results, as compiled on Wikipedia's [Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Raspberry_Award_for_Worst_Actor) page (read 10 November 2026). The 2025 and 2026 winners were cross-checked with news reports (NPR and CNN, 14 March 2026, for Ice Cube). Oscar details come from the winners' Wikipedia biographies, and Tom Cruise's honorary award from ABC News (17 November 2025).

## Corrections
- **2005, George W. Bush:** the card shows *Fahrenheit 11/9*. The correct film is ***Fahrenheit 9/11*** (2004). *Fahrenheit 11/9* (2018) is the film behind Donald Trump's 2019 win.
- **1986, Sylvester Stallone:** the card shows *Rocky IV*. The award was for both *Rambo: First Blood Part II* and *Rocky IV*.
- Several other cards show one film where the award covered two or three (Tom Arnold 1997, Bruce Willis 1999, John Travolta 2001 and 2020, Ben Affleck 2004, Ashton Kutcher 2011, Adam Sandler 2012, Donald Trump 2019). The full list is in the *Winning Film(s)* column.

## Notes
- **One entry is omitted** from this public dataset under the channel's content policy. The video shows 47 cards, and this table has 46 rows.
- The video's title says 45 years and its narration says 46. Both are correct: the ceremonies run from 1981 to 2026, which is 46 ceremonies spanning 45 years.
