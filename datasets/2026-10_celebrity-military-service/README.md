# 121 Celebrities Who Served in the Military

**Video:** [Which Famous Face Fought in WWII? Most People Get This Wrong](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-1zkgUJeRlI), published 15 October 2026
**Rows:** 121 actors, musicians, athletes, royals and business founders, in the order they appear in the video

## By the numbers
- **By branch:** Army 48 · Navy 25 · Air Force 17 · Marines 13 · Foreign forces 11 · Coast Guard 7
- **108** were born in the United States and **8** in the United Kingdom. Altogether they come from **7 countries**; the others are Austria, Germany, Israel, Jamaica and South Korea.
- **89** are classic stars, famous before 2000; **32** are modern.
- **Longest service on the list:** Jimmy Stewart, 1941 to 1968, retiring as a brigadier general in the Air Force Reserve.

## Sources
Every row cites two sources: Wikipedia plus an independent one, such as an official military history office, the National WWII Museum, Military.com, Britannica, Biography.com or a news report.

## Corrections
- **Henry Fonda:** we reworded the service detail. He enlisted in the Navy rather than make war films in a studio.
- **Chris Kyle:** we reworded the service detail. The Pentagon confirmed more than 150 kills. The 160 figure is his own account, which the Navy neither confirmed nor denied. The card shows 1998–2009, but Wikipedia gives 1999–2009.

## Notes
- **Unconfirmed rows:** J.D. Salinger, Evel Knievel, Ted Turner, Chris Cooper, Montel Williams, Ice-T, Marcus Luttrell and Alejandro Villanueva. For each, sources disagree on the years or a detail rests on one source. The Note column explains each one.
- **Colonel Sanders:** "Colonel" is an honorary Kentucky title, not a military rank. He did serve as a soldier in the US Army in 1906–1907.
- **Rob O'Neill:** his account of firing the shots in the bin Laden raid has never been officially confirmed.
- **Born abroad:** Arnold Schwarzenegger (Austrian Army), Gal Gadot (Israel Defense Forces) and Psy (South Korean Army) served in their home countries' forces. Shaggy was born in Jamaica and served in the US Marines. Werner Klemperer was born in Germany and served in the US Army.
