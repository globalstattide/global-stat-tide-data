# Hollywood's Most Profitable Actors: 100 Highest-Grossing Stars

**Video:** [Hollywood's Most Profitable Actors Are Not Who You Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ry4E_apsXpA), published 10 September 2026
**Rows:** 100 actors, from Zoe Saldaña ($15.47B) to Anne Hathaway ($4.15B), in the order they appear in the video

> **What the total measures:** the combined worldwide box office of every film in which the actor had a **leading or lead-ensemble role**. It is not the actor's earnings. A film shared by several stars counts in full for each of them, which is why the *Avengers* films lift so many totals.

## By the numbers
- **#1:** Zoe Saldaña, $15.47B, thanks to *Avatar*, *Avengers* and *Guardians of the Galaxy*. Scarlett Johansson ($15.40B) and Tom Holland ($15.20B) follow.
- **Best average per film:** Danai Gurira, $7.00B from 5 films (about $1.40B each), followed by Pom Klementieff ($6.55B from 5).
- **Most films:** Nicolas Cage, 93 films for $6.03B, and Bruce Willis, 88 films for $6.76B.
- **21 women and 79 men.** **60** of the 100 are American and **25** are British (including Scottish and Welsh).

## Corrections
- Three cards show different totals from our working file. The table uses the card values: **Chadwick Boseman** (card $7.75B, file $7.65B), **Brad Pitt** (card $7.25B, file $7.50B) and **Idris Elba** (card $7.10B, file $7.40B). The Numbers' 10 November figures for all three are higher than both.

## Notes
- **Totals change over time.** On 10 November 2026, 11 actors were more than 10% above their card total on The Numbers. The biggest gaps: Anne Hathaway ($6.68B), Matt Damon ($9.12B), Ryan Reynolds ($6.61B), Hugh Jackman ($7.91B) and Tom Hanks ($12.09B). Each one is flagged in the Note column.
- A few totals on The Numbers are now *lower* than the card (for example, Tom Holland $14.52B and Tom Cruise $12.73B). The Numbers revises its data, so totals can move in either direction. For an up-to-date figure, use the live chart.
- The Numbers' current top 100 also includes actors who are not in the video, such as Steve Carell, Tim Allen, Jason Momoa, Seth Rogen and the Chinese star Shen Teng. This dataset follows the video.
- Chadwick Boseman (died 2020) and Robin Williams (died 2014) are included for their career totals.
