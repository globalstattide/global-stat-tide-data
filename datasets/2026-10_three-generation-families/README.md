# 3 Generations of Famous Families: 50 Grandparent–Parent–Grandchild Lines

**Video:** [3 Generations of Famous Families](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZhkQ8DOvElQ), published 22 October 2026
**Rows:** 50 families, from Elvis Presley to Prince Albert II of Monaco, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | The full list, one family per row. Browse it right here on GitHub |

## What's inside
Each row is one direct bloodline: grandparent → parent → grandchild. All three people are notable in their own right.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Grandparent, Parent, Grandchild and their Roles | As shown in the video. **Maternal** means the line runs through the grandchild's mother; **Paternal** through the father |
| Born, Birth Country | Added for this dataset. Year of birth and country of birth, using today's borders |
| Known For | Added for this dataset. A short description of each person |
| Confidence | **Verified**: both links (grandparent to parent, parent to grandchild) confirmed by Wikipedia and one independent source |

## By the numbers
- **150 people in 50 families.** 11 of the grandparents are grandmothers, 24 of the parents are mothers, and 29 of the grandchildren are granddaughters.
- **24 lines run through the mother's side**, 26 through the father's.
- **Birth countries of all 150 people:** United States 111 · United Kingdom 20 · Canada 5 · Italy 4 · Jamaica 3 · Australia 3 · Ireland, Monaco, South Africa, Spain 1 each.
- **In 31 families all three generations were born in the United States.**
- **Widest gap:** Charlie Chaplin (born 1889) to his granddaughter Oona Chaplin (born 1986), 97 years. **Narrowest:** Georgia Holt (1926) to Chaz Bono (1969), 43 years.
- **Earliest-born grandparent:** Reginald Vanderbilt (1880). **Youngest grandchild:** Blue Ivy Carter (2012).
- **22 grandchildren are known by a different surname from their famous grandparent**, among them Riley Keough (Elvis Presley), Anderson Cooper (Reginald Vanderbilt), Kiefer Sutherland (Tommy Douglas) and Elon Musk (Joshua Haldeman).
