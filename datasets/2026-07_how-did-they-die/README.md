# How Did They Die? 73 Famous Figures in History

**Video:** [How Did They Die? 73 Famous Figures in History — Causes of Death Revealed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8RWHMEs177s), published 12 July 2026
**Rows:** 73 people, from Socrates to Queen Elizabeth II, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | The full list. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_How_Did_They_Die.xlsx` | The same data, a Stats sheet and a Sources sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cause of Death, Age at Death, Country | As shown in the video |
| Year of Death | Added for this dataset |
| Category | Our grouping: Illness, Violent, Accident, Natural, Suicide or Unknown |
| Certainty | **Documented**: recorded at the time. **Traditional account**: comes from ancient or religious tradition. **Disputed**: historians disagree |
| Source 1, Source 2 | The person's Wikipedia article and their Encyclopaedia Britannica article |

## By the numbers
- **Illness** 36 · **Violent death** 18 (10 assassinations, 4 executions, 3 shootings, 1 crucifixion) · **Accident** 9 · **Old age / natural** 7 · **Suicide** 2 · **Unknown** 1 (Amelia Earhart)
- **Average age at death:** 59.4. The youngest is Anne Frank (15); the oldest is Queen Elizabeth II (96).

## Sources
Encyclopaedia Britannica and cited Wikipedia biographies, checked by Global Stat Tide. Every row links both pages in the Source 1 and Source 2 columns. For ancient figures (Buddha, Confucius, Socrates, Cleopatra, Alexander, Jesus), ages and causes follow the standard historical or traditional accounts. These are flagged in the Certainty column.

## Notes
- **Anne Frank:** shown under Germany, where she was born. She lived in the Netherlands and died in the Bergen-Belsen camp.
- **Marilyn Monroe:** officially ruled a probable suicide by overdose.
- **Rasputin:** his birth year is uncertain. Britannica gives 1872 with a question mark, which would make him 44 at death; the age shown (47) assumes a birth year of 1869.
