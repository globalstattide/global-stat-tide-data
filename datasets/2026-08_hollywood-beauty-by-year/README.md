# Beauty of Hollywood, 1970–2026: One Iconic Actress per Year

**Video:** [Hollywood's Most Beautiful Actress of Each Year From 1970 to 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xk_IRcNiWJY), published 2 August 2026

> **This is an editorial selection, not a statistical ranking.** As the video says, "we selected one iconic woman per year who captured the world's attention". The facts about each actress (birth date, birthplace, status, a film from that era) were verified with two independent sources.

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | One row per year. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Hollywood_Beauty_1970_2026.xlsx` | The same data in Excel |

## Columns
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Born, Birth City, Birth Country | Birthplace, using today's country names |
| Status | Alive, or the year of death (as of November 2026) |
| A Film From That Era | One film released within about three years of the selected year. Where several fit, the alternatives are listed in the Note column |
| Confidence | **Verified**: birth date and birthplace confirmed by two sources |

## Highlights
- **Born outside the United States:** 22 of the 54 listed actresses, including Liv Ullmann (born in Tokyo), Michelle Yeoh (Malaysia), Mila Kunis (Ukraine) and Aishwarya Rai Bachchan (India). Nicole Kidman was born in Honolulu, and Anya Taylor-Joy in Miami.
- **Farrah Fawcett** (1978) is the only actress in this period's list who has died.

## Notes
- **Three years (1976, 1977, 1996) are not included** in this public dataset. The actresses shown for those years were under 18 at the time.
- Birth-city disagreements between sources are listed in the Note column (Julia Roberts: Atlanta or Smyrna; Margot Robbie: Gold Coast or Dalby; and others).
- **Sources:** Wikipedia, Britannica, Biography.com, TV Insider and other references. Each row has its own links.
