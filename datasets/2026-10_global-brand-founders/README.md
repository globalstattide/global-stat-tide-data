# The Founders Behind 120 Famous Non-American Brands

**Video:** [Surprising Founders Behind the World's Biggest Brands!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esQ75k1hCiU), published 18 October 2026
**Rows:** 120 brands, from Mercedes-Benz to VinFast, in the order they appear in the video

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Brand, Founder, Year Founded, Country | As shown in the video. Country is where the brand was founded |
| Co-Founders / Partners | Other people involved in the founding |
| Founder Born, Founder Birth Country | Added for this dataset. On two-founder cards these refer to the first-named founder. Left blank where no reliable record was found |
| Founder Gender | The main founder on the card |
| Confidence | **Verified**: founder and founding year confirmed by two independent sources |
| Note | Name changes, later brand launches and disputed dates |

**Order:** ranks 1–60 are the most recognisable brands for a US, UK and Canadian audience. Ranks 61–120 are sorted by founding year, oldest first.

**Year Founded** is the year the original company started. Where the famous name came later, the Note says so: Karl Benz founded Benz & Cie in 1883, and the Mercedes-Benz brand dates from 1926.

## By the numbers
- **24 countries.** United Kingdom 16 · Germany 15 · Japan 15 · Italy 15 · France 14 · Switzerland 5
- **Founded:** before 1800: 3 · 1800s: 38 · 1900–1949: 37 · 1950–1999: 33 · 2000 or later: 9. The median founding year is 1921.
- **Oldest:** Beretta (1526). **Newest:** VinFast (2017).
- **15 of the 110 founders with a known birthplace were born outside the brand's country.** Examples: Richard Hennessy (Ireland) for a French cognac, Antoni Patek (Poland) for a Swiss watchmaker, Sotirios Voulgaris (Greece) for Bulgari, Horacio Pagani (Argentina) for an Italian supercar, and Chung Ju-yung of Hyundai, born in what is now North Korea.
- **6 women founders:** Margarete Steiff, Melitta Bentz, Adele Fendi, Céline Vipiana, Vivienne Westwood and Cher Wang (HTC).

## Sources
Each row has two sources. Source 1 is mostly a cited Wikipedia article, cross-checked against Wikidata. Source 2 is independent: Encyclopaedia Britannica, the company's official history, or major press. Global Stat Tide checked every card in the video against this table.

## Notes
- Founding dates are often disputed. Where sources disagree we use the company's own date and give the alternatives in the Note column. Examples: Omega (1848 vs 1877), Balenciaga (1917 vs 1919), Shopify (2004 vs 2006), Dolce & Gabbana (1985 vs 1982).
- **Lindt:** the founder is David Sprüngli. The Lindt name comes from Rodolphe Lindt, whose factory the Sprüngli company bought in 1899.
- **Reebok:** founded in Bolton, England, but its headquarters are now in Boston, USA.
