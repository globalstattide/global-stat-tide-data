## By the numbers
- Together, these 50 people are worth about **$2.63 trillion**.
- **Elon Musk** ($839B) holds about **32%** of that total. He is worth 4.9 times France's Bernard Arnault, who is No. 2 on this list.
- **Three crypto fortunes** make the list: Changpeng Zhao (Canada), Giancarlo Devasini (Italy) and Jean-Louis van der Velde (Netherlands).

## Sources
Forbes *World's Billionaires 2026* (March 2026), richest person per country. The video shows a selection of 50 countries; the Forbes list covers more.

## Notes
- **Kuwait (Kutayba Alghanim, $5B):** this entry is not in the Forbes 2026 country-by-country list, and the figure could not be confirmed. It is marked *Unconfirmed*.
- **Canada:** Forbes lists Changpeng Zhao under Canada, his citizenship. He was born in China.
- **Ties:** the Collison brothers (Ireland) are tied at $17.5B each. In Switzerland, Rafaela Aponte-Diamant is tied with her husband Gianluigi Aponte.
