# The People Who Built the World's Most Iconic Brands

**Video:** [The People Who Built the World's Most Iconic Brands](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YldPKt0P8s), published 27 September 2026
**Rows:** 114 brands and their founders, from Hermès (1837) to Binance (2017), oldest first, in the order they appear in the video

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Founder, Company, Year Founded, Founder's Country of Birth | As shown in the video (the flag on each card is the founder's country of birth, not the company's home country) |
| Birthplace | Added for this dataset: town or city of birth. Country names follow today's borders |
| Confidence | **Verified** (46 rows): founder, year and country of birth confirmed by Wikipedia and an independent source (Britannica, the company's own history or the press). **Partial** (68 rows): founder and year confirmed by Wikipedia, and by Source 2 where one is given; the birthplace rests on the founder's Wikipedia biography only |
| Note | Co-founders, what the founding year refers to, and other context. "Birthplace: Wikipedia only" marks rows where Source 2 confirms the founder and year but not the birthplace |
| Source 1 | The company's English Wikipedia article |
| Source 2 | An independent page confirming the founder and founding year: the company's own history page, Britannica, another encyclopedia or a major outlet. Filled for rows 1–55; empty where the row rests on Wikipedia only |

## By the numbers
- **Founding years:** 9 brands before 1900, 84 in the 20th century, 21 since 2000.
- **Where the founders were born:** United States 67 · Germany 8 · Italy 7 · Japan 6 · China 5 · United Kingdom, France and Sweden 3 each · 12 other countries 1 each. In all, 47 of the 114 were born outside the United States.
- **German-born founders of brands built elsewhere:** Thierry Hermès (Paris), Levi Strauss (San Francisco), Henri Nestlé (Switzerland), Marcus Goldman (New York) and Hans Wilsdorf (Rolex, founded in London).
- **Women:** 3 of the 114 founders listed are women: Coco Chanel, Estée Lauder and Melanie Perkins (Canva).
- **Confidence:** 46 rows Verified, 68 Partial. Every row links its Wikipedia source; 55 rows also link an independent second source.

## Sources
The company's Wikipedia article (cited infobox and history) for the founder and founding year, and the founder's Wikipedia biography for the birthplace, all checked by Global Stat Tide in November 2026. Each row links its sources in the **Source 1** and **Source 2** columns. Second sources include Encyclopaedia Britannica, the companies' own history pages, Encyclopedia.com, the New Georgia Encyclopedia, NCpedia, Encyclopaedia Universalis, Grace's Guide, the National Sporting Goods Association Hall of Fame and the press. Rows 80, 81 and 86 were also checked against Reference for Business and Britannica; the notes cite dyson.co.uk, Yankee Magazine, Boston University, Business Insider and CNBC. A summary is in the Sources sheet.

## Corrections
- **Larry Page:** the card shows the company as **Alphabet**. The correct name is **Google**, founded in 1998. Alphabet was created in 2015 as Google's parent company.
- **Jack Dorsey:** the card shows the company as **X**. The correct name is **Twitter**, founded in 2006. It was renamed X in 2023.
- **Coco Chanel (Note column):** an earlier version of this dataset placed her 1910 shop at 31 rue Cambon. Wikipedia and Encyclopedia.com both give **21 rue Cambon**; the house moved to No. 31 later in the 1910s.

## Notes
- **What "Year Founded" means:** many brands began under another name or as a smaller business. The Note column says what each year refers to. For example, Pepsi is dated from the 1902 company (the drink dates from 1893), KFC from its first franchise in 1952, and Chick-fil-A from its first restaurant in 1967.
- **Dyson:** shown as 1993, the year Dyson's first own-brand vacuum (the DC01) launched. The company was incorporated in 1991.
- **Jensen Huang:** born in Taiwan. Britannica gives Tainan as his birthplace; Wikipedia gives Taipei.
- **Jerry Murrell and Tim Sweeney:** both American. Their birth towns were not found in the sources checked, so the Birthplace cell is empty.
- **Giorgio Armani** died in September 2025.
