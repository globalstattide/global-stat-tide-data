# Celebrity Relationships: From Ex to Next

**Video:** [Celebrity Relationships That Shocked the World — From Ex to Next](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GrwhcgLqMiI), published 17 August 2026
**Rows:** 48 celebrities, from Taylor Swift to Brent Rivera, numbered in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | Every celebrity with their ex, their partner on the card and where things stood in November 2026. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Celebrity_Ex_to_Next.xlsx` | The same data colour-coded by confidence, plus a Stats sheet and a Method sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Video #, Celebrity, Profession, Ex, Current | As shown on the video card |
| Status (Nov 2026) | Where the relationship stood when we re-checked it, three months after the video was published |
| Confidence | **Verified** (20 rows): two independent 2026 sources confirm the status. **Partial** (26): one source, or a long-established couple we did not re-check. **Unconfirmed** (2): sources conflict or none found |
| Note | Changes since the video, and exes that were reported romances rather than confirmed relationships |

## By the numbers
- **18** of the 48 couples on the cards are married and **2** are engaged.
- **23** are together, including 3 reported couples who have not confirmed it.
- **3** have split, all reported after the video was published: Elon Musk and Shivon Zilis, Vinicius Junior and Virginia Fonseca, and Dakota Johnson and Tucker Pillsbury (Role Model).
- **21** of the 48 celebrities are actors or actresses, **7** are singers, **5** are footballers and **5** are models.

## Sources
Hello!, Hola!, W Magazine, Rolling Stone, Variety, Marie Claire, Goal, SCMP and other outlets, plus Wikipedia, checked by Global Stat Tide in November 2026. Links are in every row.

## Changes since the video
- **Elon Musk and Shivon Zilis** split in October 2026.
- **Vinicius Junior and Virginia Fonseca** split in May 2026, got back together in June and split again in October 2026.
- **Dakota Johnson and Tucker Pillsbury (Role Model):** a split was reported on 20 August 2026, three days after the video.
- **Nicole Kidman:** she was linked to Michael Reinstein in July 2026 reports. In September a report said the romance had cooled. Neither has confirmed a relationship, so the status is "Unclear".
- **Ariana Grande:** split from Ethan Slater in June 2026. A renewed romance with her 2013 boyfriend Ricky Alvarez was reported but not confirmed.

## Corrections
- **Sergey Brin:** the card prints his partner's name as Gerelyn "Gilbert-Soto". The correct form is **Gerelyn Gilbert-Soto**.
- **Brent Rivera:** we found no reliable source for a partner named "Monako", so this row is marked Unconfirmed.

## Notes
- Two cards from the video are left out of this dataset. In both, the "ex" relationship shown dates from when one of the people was under 18.
- Several "exes" were reported romances that were never confirmed by both people (for example Zendaya and Jacob Elordi, Gigi Hadid and Leonardo DiCaprio). The Note column says so where it applies.
- Long-established married couples were not re-checked one by one; they are marked Partial.
