## Highlights
- **Four first-time qualifiers:** Curaçao, Cape Verde, Jordan and Uzbekistan.
- **Most appearances:** Brazil, 23, the only team to play at every World Cup.
- **Titles in the field:** 18 of the 22 World Cups ever played were won by teams in this tournament. Italy's 4 are the missing ones.

## Sources
FIFA World Cup 2026 final draw (5 December 2025), the March 2026 play-offs, and FIFA tournament records. Appearances include 2026.

## Corrections
- **Germany:** the card shows 5 titles. Germany has **4** (1954, 1974, 1990, 2014).
- **Uruguay:** the card shows 0 titles. Uruguay has **2** (1930, 1950).
