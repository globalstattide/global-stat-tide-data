# Worst Actress Razzie Winners, 1981–2026

**Video:** [The Worst Actress Razzie Winners, Ranked by Who Actually Deserved It](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xSlkAtJGxk), published 17 September 2026
**Rows:** 46 Golden Raspberry (Razzie) ceremonies, from the first in 1981 to 2026, in the order they appear in the video

> **The "who actually deserved it" framing in the title is editorial opinion, not a measured ranking.** The video shows the winners in ceremony order, and this dataset records only the award facts: who won, for which film, and when. Those facts come from the Razzie records.

| Tie Co-Winner | Filled in for the two ceremonies that ended in a tie |
| Oscar Winner? | Whether the winner also holds an Academy Award |

## By the numbers
- **Most wins:** Madonna 5 (including one tie), Bo Derek 3 (including one tie). Pia Zadora, Demi Moore, Sharon Stone, Tyler Perry and Dakota Johnson won twice each.
- **Oscar winners who also won this Razzie:** Liza Minnelli (Cabaret), Halle Berry (Monster's Ball) and Sandra Bullock (The Blind Side, the night after her Razzie). Tyler Perry, who won twice for playing Madea, received the Academy's honorary Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award in 2021.
- **Not an actress:** Adam Sandler (2012) won for playing a woman. Tyler Perry (2014, 2018) did too.
- **8 wins** covered more than one film. **2 ceremonies** (1982, 2003) ended in a tie.

## Sources
Golden Raspberry Award Foundation results, as listed on Wikipedia's "Golden Raspberry Award for Worst Actress" page and checked against news reports for the most recent ceremonies (Cinema Stance for 2024, Variety for 2026). Oscar facts come from Academy Awards records.

## Corrections
- **1982:** the card shows Bo Derek (Tarzan, the Ape Man) only. She **tied with Faye Dunaway** (Mommie Dearest).
- **2003:** the card shows Britney Spears (Crossroads) only. She **tied with Madonna** (Swept Away).
- **2024:** the card shows Megan Fox for **Expend4bles**. Her Worst Actress Razzie was for **Johnny & Clyde**. Expend4bles won her Worst Supporting Actress the same night.

## Notes
- **2023:** no performer won. The Razzies withdrew their nomination of a 12-year-old actress after criticism and gave the award to themselves. We do not name the child.
- **1981:** Brooke Shields was 14 when The Blue Lagoon was made.
- **2011:** the card says "Entire Cast". The award went to the four lead actresses of Sex and the City 2.
