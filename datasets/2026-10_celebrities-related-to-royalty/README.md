## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Relationship (video) | The label on the card. "Gens Apart" means "times removed": the two people sit that many generations apart on the family tree |
| Exact Degree | The precise relationship as the source publishes it. Where sources disagree, both are given |
| Link Type | **Genealogical research** (54): a distant blood link traced by genealogists. **Close family** (20): parent, grandparent, sibling, aunt or uncle. **Marriage** (20): related by marriage only. Meghan Markle counts as both marriage and research |
| Traced By / Basis | Who found or published the link, for example NEHGS / American Ancestors (Gary Boyd Roberts, Christopher Child), Ancestry.com, PBS *Finding Your Roots*, *Who Do You Think You Are?* or the University of Leicester |
| Quote 1, Quote 2 | The sentence in each source that states the link |
| Confidence | **Verified** (72): two sources, each with a quoted sentence. **Single source** (23): one source |

## By the numbers
- **Relatives:** 32 US presidents, 27 monarchs, 15 other royals, 13 nobles, 4 prime ministers, 3 foreign presidents and 1 provincial premier.
- **Most connected:** Queen Elizabeth II (15 celebrities), then John F. Kennedy and Princess Diana (6 each), George W. Bush (5), and Barack Obama and Ronald Reagan (4 each).
- **53 women, 42 men.** 68 of the celebrities were born in the US and 15 in the UK.

## Sources
Distant links come from published genealogical research, mostly by the New England Historic Genealogical Society (NEHGS / American Ancestors) and Ancestry.com, as reported by NPR, AP, NBC News, CBS News, the New York Times, the University of Leicester, PBS, Countryfile, Marie Claire and Hello!. Close family and marriage links come from cited Wikipedia biographies and Britannica. Every row links its sources and quotes the key sentence.

## Corrections
- **Redfoo (No. 91):** the card shows "2nd Cousin, 1 Gen Apart" of Jimmy Carter. Strictly, he is a **half**-second cousin once removed: the Gordy and Carter families share one great-grandfather through different mothers. The same applies to his father Berry Gordy (No. 45), whose card correctly says "Half-2nd Cousin".

## Notes
- **Probable links:** Marilyn Monroe's link to George W. Bush runs through her presumed but unproven father, and Elvis Presley's link to Jimmy Carter is described by the genealogists as "probably" sixth cousins once removed. Both are marked "Probably" in the Exact Degree column.
- **Degree differs between sources** for Richard Gere and Céline Dion, so the video shows "Distant Cousin". Both readings are in the Exact Degree column.
- **Very distant cousins** (10th cousin and beyond) are common: most people of European descent share medieval royal ancestors. These links are presented as published, not independently re-proven.
- **Same original story:** some "Verified" rows rest on one wire report carried by two outlets (for example Brad Pitt and Barack Obama, AP 2008). The Note column says so.
- **Not a blood relative:** Arnold Schwarzenegger, Chris Pratt and Cheryl Hines married into the Kennedy family. Zsa Zsa Gabor's husband got his "Prince" title by adult adoption.
