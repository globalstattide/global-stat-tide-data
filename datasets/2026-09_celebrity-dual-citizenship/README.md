# Celebrities With More Than One Citizenship

**Video:** [Hollywood's Secret Passports: Who Holds More Than One Country?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgjMief6I-4), published 24 September 2026
**Rows:** 100 actors, musicians, athletes and public figures, in the order they appear in the video

> **Only legal citizenship counts.** As the video says, "having a grandparent from somewhere didn't count". Ancestry and heritage alone are not citizenship.

## By the numbers
- **8 people hold three citizenships:** Dua Lipa, Liam Neeson, Christoph Waltz, Ana de Armas, Joel Embiid, Elon Musk, Steven Seagal and Kim Cattrall. The other 92 hold two.
- **United States:** 83 of the 100 hold US citizenship (including Wayne Gretzky, which is disputed; see below). Next come the United Kingdom 29, Canada 24 and Ireland 8.
- **Birth countries:** United Kingdom 23, United States 22, Canada 20.
- **17 hold no US citizenship at all,** among them Lionel Messi, Shakira, Colin Firth, Idris Elba and Sophia Loren.

## Sources
Each row lists its own sources: Wikipedia biographies (cited entries), People, NPR, CBS News, NBA.com, The Moscow Times and others. 20 rows give "Global Stat Tide heritage research": these facts came from our earlier research for the Ethnicity video and have no single link. Many US naturalizations were cross-checked against Wikipedia's list of naturalized American citizens.

## Corrections
- **Kim Cattrall:** the card shows Canada and the UK. She also **became a US citizen in 2020**, so she holds three citizenships (Wikipedia; ET Online).
- **Wayne Gretzky:** the card shows Canada and the USA. In 2025 Gretzky said: "I never became an American citizen. I'm Canadian." (Global News). Older reports say he became a US citizen. Marked **Unconfirmed**.

## Unconfirmed rows
We could not confirm the second citizenship for these 6 people. They appear in the video, so they stay in the table, marked **Unconfirmed**:
- **Lenny Kravitz** (Bahamas): he applied in 2023; we found no report that it was granted.
- **Henry Golding** (Malaysia): Wikipedia lists British citizenship only.
- **Billie Eilish** (Ireland): Irish ancestry is documented; citizenship is not.
- **Shakira** (Spain) and **Sandra Bullock** (Germany): widely reported, not officially confirmed.
- **Wayne Gretzky:** see Corrections.

**Catherine Zeta-Jones** (US) is marked **Partial**: secondary sources only.

## Notes
- Salma Hayek's French citizenship (listed on Wikipedia) and Anya Taylor-Joy's Argentine citizenship are not counted. We could not confirm either.
- Michael Douglas holds Bermudian status through his mother. Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory.
