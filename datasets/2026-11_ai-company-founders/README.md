# The Founders of the World's Biggest AI Companies: 70 Companies Ranked by Value

**Video:** [Founders of World's Biggest AI Companies!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIlUfKRtFBw), published 5 November 2026
**Rows:** 70 AI companies and one founder each, ranked from the smallest value (Pika, $470M) to the largest (Nvidia, $5.2T), as in the video. Values as of 28 October 2026

## By the numbers
- Together the 70 companies are worth about **$8.9 trillion**. **Nvidia alone ($5.25T) is 59%** of that.
- **6 companies** are worth $100 billion or more: Nvidia, Anthropic ($965B), OpenAI ($852B), Palantir ($478B), xAI ($250B) and Databricks ($190B). The median company is worth **$8.8 billion**.
- **37 of the 70** were founded in 2022 or later.
- **Headquarters:** 45 in the US, 8 in China, 6 in the UK, 3 in Germany and 2 in India.

## Sources
Company announcements, Bloomberg, Reuters, TechCrunch, SiliconANGLE, the Financial Times, CNBC, Fortune and the companies' law firms for funding rounds; SEC filings and deal announcements for acquisitions; StockAnalysis, CompaniesMarketCap and Google Finance for market capitalisations. Hong Kong and Chinese market caps are converted to US dollars. Every row links its sources and quotes the key sentence.

## Notes
- **Market caps move every day.** The nine listed companies (Nvidia, Palantir, CoreWeave, Zhipu AI, Cerebras, iFlytek, MiniMax, SenseTime, C3.ai) are shown at their late-October 2026 values.
- **Deals not yet closed:** Nvidia's purchase of Hugging Face ($12.9B) and AMD's purchase of World Labs ($8.2B) were announced but still pending.
- **Manus ($2B)** is the price of Meta's December 2025 deal, which was reversed on China's order. Manus now operates on its own, so this is not a current valuation.
- **Talks, not closed rounds:** Isomorphic Labs ($40B), Poolside ($12B) and Magic ($1.5B) come from reported fundraising talks. OpenEvidence ($15B) comes from one press report; its last company-confirmed valuation was $12B.
- **Older figures:** 12 companies, among them Stability AI (2022), Jasper (2022) and Inflection AI (2023), have not disclosed a newer valuation. Their real value today may be different.
- **Anthropic** filed for an IPO on 26 October 2026. It had not priced when this dataset was published (10 November 2026).
