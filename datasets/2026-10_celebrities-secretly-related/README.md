# 114 Celebrities Who Are Secretly Related

**Video:** [114 Celebrities Who Are Secretly Related](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grPJ7YULSpM), published 1 October 2026
**Rows:** 114 family connections, one per card, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Famous Person, Relationship, Related Famous Person | As shown in the video (one correction, see below) |
| Relationship Type | Our grouping: Parent and child, Siblings, Aunt/uncle and niece/nephew, Grandparent and beyond, Cousins, By marriage, Step-family, Adoptive |
| How They Are Connected | The family link behind the card, usually why the surnames don't match |
| Sources | The two references used for that row in our research |
| Confidence | **Verified**: two sources re-checked and in agreement. **Partial**: documented in the references listed, but not re-opened in the final check |
| Card Correction | Where the card in the video was wrong |

## By the numbers
- **Parent and child:** 49 of the 114 cards (28 "daughter of", 20 "son of", 1 "secret daughter of"). Then siblings 17, aunt/uncle and niece/nephew 14, grandparents and great-grandparents 11, by marriage 10, step-family 7, cousins 5, adoption 1.
- **207 different people** appear on the 114 cards. John F. Kennedy and Jamie Lee Curtis each appear on 3 cards.

## Sources
Global Stat Tide research from September 2026, using cited Wikipedia entries, Encyclopaedia Britannica and news outlets (NYT, AP, BBC, People, TODAY and others). Each row names its two references. In November 2026 we re-checked 14 rows against Wikipedia, including every card that was wrong or simplified.

## Corrections
- **Chris Pratt:** the card shows "Grandson-in-Law of John F. Kennedy". The correct relationship is **great-nephew by marriage**. Pratt is married to Katherine Schwarzenegger, whose grandmother Eunice Kennedy Shriver was JFK's sister.

## Notes
- **Kate Hudson:** the card says "Stepdaughter of Kurt Russell". Russell is her mother Goldie Hawn's long-time partner and raised her, but Hawn and Russell never married and he did not adopt her.
- **Lenny Kravitz and Al Roker:** the card says "Cousin of". Wikipedia describes them as second cousins, through Kravitz's mother Roxie Roker; sources differ on the exact degree.
- **Sabrina Carpenter:** Nancy Cartwright confirmed in 2024 that Carpenter is her niece. According to Wikipedia, Carpenter's father is Cartwright's stepbrother.
- **Recent deaths:** Chip Taylor (March 2026), Robert Carradine (February 2026) and George Wendt (May 2025).
- The research also produced lists of unproven rumours and debunked claims. They were not shown as cards and are not part of this dataset.
