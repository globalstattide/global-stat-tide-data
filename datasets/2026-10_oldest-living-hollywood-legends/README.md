# Hollywood Legends Still Alive in 2026: 120 Screen Stars

**Video:** [The Oldest Hollywood Legends Are Still Going Strong!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bXU589a_PyQ), published 25 October 2026
**Rows:** 120 actors and actresses, from Clint Eastwood to Mickey Rourke, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | The full list. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Oldest_Living_Hollywood_Legends.xlsx` | The same data, plus a Stats sheet (live formulas) and a Method sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Age, Born | As shown in the video. Age is in whole years on 25 October 2026, the day the video was published |
| Birth Date, Country of Birth | Added for this dataset. Country of Birth is where the person was born, not the nationality they are known by |
| Known For, Awards | Three signature roles and a short awards summary |
| Status (10 Nov 2026) | Re-checked after publication against 2026 news death lists |
| Alive Evidence (2025-26) | A dated news item showing the person alive, found before the video was made |
| Confidence | **Verified** (all 120 rows): birth data from Wikipedia plus a second source (usually Britannica), and no death report at the time of the video |

## By the numbers
- **65 men, 55 women.** Average age on publication day: **82.7**.
- **27** were 90 or older, **50** were in their 80s and **43** were under 80.
- **Oldest:** Dick Van Dyke and Mel Brooks, both 100. **Youngest:** Michael J. Fox, 65.
- **96** were born in the United States and **13** in the United Kingdom.

## Sources
Wikipedia biographies and Encyclopaedia Britannica for birth dates and birthplaces, plus a dated 2025-26 news report for each person (links in every row). Status re-checked on 10 November 2026 against the Fox 29 "Celebrity deaths of 2026" list (updated 9 November 2026) and AOL's "Celebrity deaths 2026" list.

## Corrections
- **Patrick Stewart (No. 69)** died in Los Angeles on **28 October 2026**, three days after the video was published (ITV News, Variety). He was alive when the video went out. His row is kept, and the Status column shows the date.

## Notes
- **Born outside the country they are known by:** Bruce Willis (West Germany), Julie Christie (British India), Ann-Margret (Sweden). Rita Moreno is listed under Puerto Rico, a US territory.
- **Birthdays after the video:** Jaclyn Smith turned 81 on 26 October and June Squibb turned 97 on 6 November. The ages above are as of 25 October 2026.
