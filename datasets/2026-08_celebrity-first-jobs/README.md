# Celebrities' First Jobs: 118 Stars Before They Were Famous

**Video:** [Celebrities' First Jobs Will Shock You - Brad Pitt Was a Chicken Mascot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h_zjS6qlV5k), published 9 August 2026
**Rows:** 118 people, from Sylvester Stallone to Kate Hudson, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | Every card with what the sources actually say. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Celebrity_First_Jobs.xlsx` | The same data colour-coded by confidence, plus a Stats sheet and a Method sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| First Job (card) | The job exactly as written on the video card |
| Check | **Confirmed**: sources support the card. **Partly**: sources support a related job, or it wasn't the person's first job. **Corrected**: sources describe a different job. **Not confirmed**: we found no reliable source either way |
| Documented Early Job | What the sources actually say |
| Confidence | For the Documented Early Job column. **Verified**: two independent sources. **Partial**: one source. **Unconfirmed**: nothing reliable found |

## By the numbers
- **79** card jobs are confirmed by sources, **10** are partly right, **15** are corrected below and **14** could not be confirmed.
- The documented jobs are **Verified** for 49 people, **Partial** for 55 and **Unconfirmed** for 14.
- **15** cards show a food or drink job (waiting tables, bartending, bussing, washing dishes, barista or cook). **4** show an ice cream job, **4** show lifeguard and **4** show model.

## Sources
Wikipedia biographies, Biography.com, Woman & Home, The Jerusalem Post, lovemoney, E! Online, Romper, CinemaBlend, Eat This, Not That!, Cheapism, NBC, NPR, SCMP and other outlets, checked by Global Stat Tide in November 2026. Links are in every row.

## Notes
- **Partly right:** Cardi B's first job was as a supermarket cashier, before she worked as a dancer. Angelina Jolie studied to become a funeral director; no source says she worked at a funeral home. Rowan Atkinson studied electrical engineering but never worked as an engineer. See the Note column for the other seven.
- **Jesus:** "carpenter" comes from the traditional account (Gospel of Mark 6:3).
- Our working file had four more names (Eva Green, David Murdock, Ashley Tisdale, Charlie Chaplin) that did not make the final video. They are not included here.

- **One card from the video is not included**, under the channel's content policy.
