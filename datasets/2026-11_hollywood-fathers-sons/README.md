# Hollywood Fathers vs. Sons: 72 Father–Son Pairs Ranked by Popularity

**Video:** [Hollywood Fathers vs. Sons: Who's More Famous Now?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SLyA8O87h7U), published 12 November 2026
**Rows:** 72 father–son pairs, from Kirk and Michael Douglas to Bill and Lewis Pullman, in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | The full list, one pair per row. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Hollywood_Fathers_Sons.xlsx` | The same data, a Stats sheet and a Sources & Method sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Father, Son, Relationship | As shown in the video. One son (Sean Astin) is adopted |
| Son Born, Son Birth City, Son Birth Country | Added for this dataset. Country of birth, not nationality |
| Son Best Known For, Father Best Known For | One editorial pick per person, shortened to fit the card. The card shows the work of whoever is more popular |
| Father / Son Wikipedia Views (12 months) | English Wikipedia user page views, November 2025 – October 2026, from the Wikimedia Pageviews API |
| Son Share % | The son's views as a share of the pair's combined views. This is the needle on the video's popularity gauge |
| More Popular Today | Whoever had more views over the 12 months |
| Fact | The surprising fact used in the video's research |
| Confidence | **Verified**: the family link and the fact confirmed by two sources. **Unconfirmed**: one source |

## By the numbers
- **The sons win 38 of the 72 pairs; the fathers win 34.**
- **Biggest landslides:** Chuck Norris drew 21 times as many views as his son Eric; Micky Dolenz 14 times his father George; Mel Gibson 11 times his son Milo; Dean Stockwell 11 times his father Harry; Bruce Lee 10 times his son Brandon.
- **Closest calls:** Dennis and Jack Quaid (son 49.8%), Donald and Kiefer Sutherland (49.4%), Desi Arnaz and Desi Arnaz Jr. (49.1%).
- **Five fathers appear with more than one son:** John Carradine (3), Lloyd Bridges, Martin Sheen, Stellan Skarsgård and Arnold Schwarzenegger (2 each).
- **64 of the 72 sons were born in the United States.**

## Sources
Wikipedia (cited entries), Encyclopaedia Britannica, Biography.com, the Academy Awards and Television Academy databases, and major news outlets. Each row lists its own links. Popularity comes from the Wikimedia Pageviews API.

## Notes
- **Sean Harmon** has no English Wikipedia article, so his father Mark Harmon is counted as more popular.
- **Birthplace not confirmed** for Joseph Baena, Milo Gibson and Luke Murray; those cells are left blank.
- **Facts about health, criminal matters and private family matters are omitted** from this public table (5 rows). The pairs themselves are included.
- Wikipedia views reflect attention in the past year, not career achievement. News events, such as a death, can raise a person's views for a few months.
