# Golden Visa Costs Ranked: The Cheapest vs the Most Expensive (2026)

**Video:** [Golden Visa Costs Ranked: The Cheapest vs. Most Expensive (2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYA_nUJKEHI), published 23 July 2026
**Rows:** 13 active residency-by-investment programmes, ranked from the cheapest minimum investment to the most expensive

## Highlights
- **Cheapest:** Latvia, from about **$57,000** (€50k company equity plus a €10k state fee).
- **Most expensive:** Singapore, about **$7.8 million** (SGD 10M business investment).
- **Fastest:** the UAE (2–8 weeks) and Panama (30–45 days). **Slowest:** the USA EB-5 (24–48 months) and Portugal (12–24 months in practice).
- **No route to citizenship:** Thailand and the UAE.

## Method
- Each programme is shown at its **lowest qualifying investment**. Many offer several tiers; these are listed in the Investment Routes column.
- Prices are converted to USD at the official reference rates for late March 2026 (EUR/USD 1.1484; AED/USD 0.27228).
- **Visa-free destinations** measure the strength of that country's passport: Henley Passport Index 2026 for most rows, and the Arton Passport Index for Latvia and Bulgaria.
- Programmes that grant **citizenship directly**, such as Turkey's, are excluded. Programmes now **closed** are also excluded: Spain (2025), Ireland (2023) and the UK (2022).
- Every figure is backed by at least two independent sources, linked in the Excel file. Research was done in July 2026; rules in this area change often.

## Corrections
- **Malta:** the video card shows a 5-year path to citizenship. The residency programme gives **no direct path**; naturalisation requires genuine residence.
- **Italy:** the card shows 184 visa-free destinations. Henley 2026 gives **185**.
