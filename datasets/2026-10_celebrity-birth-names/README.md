# The Birth Names of 123 Famous Stars

**Video:** [The Birth Names Hollywood Doesn't Want You To Know!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AQaQ1ZnSCcM), published 11 October 2026
**Rows:** 123 singers, actors, athletes and TV hosts, from Lady Gaga to Ralph Lauren, in the order they appear in the video

|---|---|

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Known Name, Birth Name, Country of Birth | As shown in the video. The country is where the person was born, using today's country name, so Freddie Mercury (born in Zanzibar) is listed under Tanzania |
| Profession | What the person is mainly known for |
| Confidence | **Verified** (111): two sources agree on the full birth name. **Partial** (10): one source confirms the full name, or the sources spell part of it differently. **Unconfirmed** (2): sources disagree |
| Note | Context, such as where the person was born or when a name is real after all, and where the two sources differ |
| Source 1 | The person's English Wikipedia article |
| Source 2 | An independent page that states the birth name: Britannica (82 rows), Biography.com (33) or another encyclopedia or news outlet (6). Blank for Raquel Welch and Stone Cold Steve Austin, where we have not yet confirmed a second page |

## By the numbers
- The 123 stars were born in **18 countries**: **89** in the United States, **14** in the United Kingdom, **4** in Canada and **2** in Puerto Rico.
- **The longest birth name:** Kiefer Sutherland's, with seven words: Kiefer William Frederick Dempsey George Rufus Sutherland.
- **Hiding in plain sight:** for Madonna, Prince and Billie Eilish, the famous name is their real first name. For Drake and Rihanna, it is their real middle name.

## Sources
Encyclopaedia Britannica, cited Wikipedia biographies, Biography.com and news obituaries. Every row was checked against two sources in October 2026, and the riskiest rows were re-checked for this release in November 2026. Each row now links its two sources in the `Source 1` and `Source 2` columns.

## Corrections
No card was found to be wrong. Sources disagree on part of 12 names, and all 12 are flagged in the table.

**Unconfirmed (sources disagree):**
- **Cher:** the card shows Cheryl Sarkisian, which matches Wikipedia. Britannica gives **Cherilyn** Sarkisian.
- **Natalie Portman:** the card shows Neta-Lee Hershlag, which matches the Jewish Women's Archive. Wikipedia and Britannica give **Natalie** Hershlag, and Portman has said her first name was always Natalie.

**Partial (spelling differs, or only one source has the full name):**
- **Demi Moore:** the card shows Demetria Gene Guynes. Britannica confirms Demetria Guynes, without the middle name Gene.
- **Omar Sharif:** the card shows Michel Dimitri Chalhoub. Britannica spells it Michel Demitri Chalhoub (or Shalhoub). Wikipedia adds a further middle name, Yusef.
- **Dr. Dre:** Britannica gives the middle name Romelle, as on the card. Wikipedia spells it Romell.
- **Helen Mirren:** Wikipedia gives Ilyena, as on the card. Britannica and Biography.com spell it **Ilynea**.
- **Cary Grant:** Wikipedia gives Archibald **Alec** Leach, as on the card. Britannica gives Archibald **Alexander** Leach.
- **Doris Day:** Wikipedia gives Doris Mary Anne Kappelhoff, as on the card. Britannica and Biography.com give Doris **von** Kappelhoff.
- **Halle Berry:** Wikipedia gives Maria Halle Berry, as on the card. Biography.com gives only her current name, Halle Maria Berry.
- **Mel Brooks, Sophia Loren, The Undertaker:** the second source confirms the name but not every middle name on the card (James; Costanza Brigida; William).

## Notes
- **Minor differences, still Verified:** Britannica orders Snoop Dogg's names Cordozar Calvin, spells Nicolas Cage's first name Nicholas and gives John Wayne the middle name Michael; for all three, Wikipedia and Biography.com match the card. Britannica also gives longer forms that contain the card name for Madonna (adding Veronica, her confirmation name) and Ralph Lauren (adding Rueben).
- **Vin Diesel:** Britannica gives his birth name as Mark Sinclair, as on the card. Wikipedia adds Vincent, his stepfather's surname.
- **Born abroad:** several stars were born outside the country they are known for. Examples are Bruce Willis (West Germany), Kiefer Sutherland and Slash (London), Engelbert Humperdinck (India), Freddie Mercury (Zanzibar) and Audrey Hepburn (Brussels).
