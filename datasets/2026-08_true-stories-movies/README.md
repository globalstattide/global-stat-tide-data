# 137 Unbelievable True Stories Turned Into Movies

**Video:** [137 Unbelievable True Stories Turned Into Movies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hwJkvO-SPYg), published 24 August 2026
**Rows:** 135 real people, from Michael Jackson to Leonard Bernstein, in the order they appear in the video (two of the 137 cards are not included, see Notes)

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Person, Country, Film or Series | As shown in the video. Country is the flag on the card |
| Release Year, Played By | Added for this dataset: year of release and the lead actor in the title role |
| Format | Feature film, Documentary, Documentary series or TV film |
| Category | Our grouping: Arts & Entertainment, Sport, Politics, Science, Civil Rights, Military, Business, Crime, Exploration, Royalty, Religion |
| Confidence | **Verified**: subject, title, actor and year confirmed. **Partial**: the film is inspired by the person rather than a direct biopic |
| Source | The page each row was checked against |

## By the numbers
- **Arts & Entertainment** 39 · **Sport** 20 · **Politics** 14 · **Science** 12 · **Civil Rights** 11 · **Military** 8 · **Business** 8 · **Crime** 8 · **Exploration** 6 · **Royalty** 5 · **Religion** 4
- **124 feature films**, 8 documentaries, 2 TV films and 1 documentary series (*The Last Dance*).
- **76 of the 135 people are American**; 17 are British, 7 French and 6 Indian.
- **Release years run from 1943** (*Madame Curie*) **to 2026** (*Michael*). 69 of the productions came out in 2010 or later; 9 before 1980.
- **Leonardo DiCaprio and Will Smith each play three real people:** DiCaprio as Howard Hughes, Frank Abagnale and Jordan Belfort; Smith as Richard Williams, Muhammad Ali and Chris Gardner.

## Sources
Every film, its subject and its lead actor were checked against Wikipedia's *List of biographical films* for the decade of release. Documentaries, TV films and films missing from those lists were checked against their own Wikipedia articles. Checked by Global Stat Tide, November 2026.

## Corrections
- **Vincent van Gogh:** the card shows a German flag. Van Gogh was **Dutch** (born in Zundert, Netherlands). The table uses the Netherlands.

## Notes
- **Two entries are not included** in this public dataset under the channel's content policy.
- **Country** is the flag on the card: the person's nationality or the country they are best known for. Where this differs from the birthplace, the Note column says so. Examples: Marie Antoinette (born in Vienna), Marie Curie (born in Warsaw), Golda Meir (born in Kyiv), Nikola Tesla (born in today's Croatia), Mother Teresa (born in Skopje), Freddie Mercury (born in Zanzibar).
- **Jesus Christ:** the card shows the flag of modern Israel. According to tradition he was born in Bethlehem, Judea, under Roman rule.
- **Guru (2007)** is widely seen as inspired by Dhirubhai Ambani, but director Mani Ratnam has called it a work of fiction. It is marked Partial.
- The cards use shortened titles for some films (for example *Long Walk to Freedom*). The table gives the full titles.
