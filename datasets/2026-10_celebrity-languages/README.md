# How Many Languages Do the Stars Really Speak? (60 Celebrities)

**Video:** [Surprising Language Skills of the World's Biggest Stars](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aTqZkTuzs-g), published 8 October 2026
**Rows:** 60 stars, in the order they appear in the video (from 2 languages up to 8)

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Country of Birth | The flag shown on the video card. It is the country of **birth**, so Anya Taylor-Joy is listed under the United States, Lupita Nyong'o under Mexico and Daniel Brühl under Spain |
| Languages (card), Count (card) | As shown in the video |
| Spoken Count (checked) | The card count minus any language a source says the person does not actually speak. Left blank for Rosalía, where no exact figure exists |
| Confidence | **Verified** (31): two independent sources. **Partial** (25): one source, or some languages are documented only as sung or acted. **Unconfirmed** (4): we could not document at least one listed language |
| Note | Context, including languages people are often credited with that we did not count |

## By the numbers
- **36 languages** appear across the 60 cards (Swiss German, Egyptian Arabic and Ancient Greek are listed separately).
- **English** is on 59 cards. The only card without it is Dalida's. **French** is on 36, **Spanish** 31, **Italian** 24, **German** 12.
- **Average:** 3.88 languages per card. 15 stars have 2; Rosalía has the most, with 8.
- The stars were born in **26 countries**. The United States leads with 9, then Italy and Spain with 5 each.

## Sources
Cited Wikipedia biographies, interviews and news reports (including AP), checked by Global Stat Tide. A language only counts as "spoken" when the person has used it in an interview, a speech or a role. Singing in a language does not count as speaking it.

## Corrections
The video's cards count some languages that the person doesn't actually speak. The narration flagged several of these. The `Spoken Count (checked)` column shows the corrected figure:
- **Sandra Bullock:** the card shows 3. The correct count is **2** (English, German). Her Mandarin was a scripted Oscars joke.
- **Christoph Waltz:** the card shows 4. The correct count is **3**. He has said he does not speak Italian; he memorised his Italian scene.
- **Mila Kunis:** the card shows 3. The correct count is **2** (Russian, English). She has said she does not speak Ukrainian.
- **Giannis Antetokounmpo:** the card shows 3. The correct count is **2** (Greek, English). We found no source that he speaks Yoruba. His mother spoke Igbo at home, and he says he understands only a little of it.
- **Monica Bellucci:** the card shows 4. The correct count is **3** (Italian, French, English). She learned her Aramaic for one role. She is also proficient in Portuguese and Spanish, which the card leaves out.
- **Shakira:** the card shows 7. The correct count is **6**. She sings in Arabic but does not speak it in interviews.
- **Rosalía:** the card shows 8. She sings in all of them but, as the video says, speaks about three.

## Notes
- **Unconfirmed rows:** Rafael Nadal (French), Salma Hayek (French, Arabic), Cristiano Ronaldo (Italian, Arabic) and Andrea Bocelli (German, Latin). We could not find a reliable source showing that they speak these languages.
- **Sung or performed only:** for Plácido Domingo, Dalida, Andrea Bocelli and Celine Dion, some listed languages are documented only as languages they sing in.
- **Often credited, not counted:** Roger Federer (Spanish), Jackie Chan (Spanish), Tom Hiddleston (Italian).
