# Celebrity Net Worth: 107 Stars and Where Their Money Really Comes From

**Video:** [Your Favorite Celebrity's Net Worth Is Not What You Think!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40MBQcmzPjI), published 1 November 2026
**Rows:** 107 actors, musicians, athletes, TV hosts and directors, ranked from the smallest fortune (Jimmy Kimmel, $50M) to the largest (Steven Spielberg, $7.9B), as in the video

| Confidence | **Verified** (45 rows): a Forbes figure, or two independent estimates within about 25% of each other. **Estimate** (62 rows): one source only, or the second source differs by more than 25% |

**Every net worth figure here is a published estimate, not an audited number.**

## By the numbers
- Together these 107 people are worth about **$72.4 billion**. The average is **$676 million**; the median is **$400 million**.
- **20 billionaires**, led by Steven Spielberg ($7.9B), George Lucas ($5.4B) and Michael Jordan ($4.7B).
- **Musicians** hold the most money in total ($18.8B), ahead of directors ($15.8B) and athletes ($15.0B). Only four directors are on the list, so they average almost $4 billion each.
- **62 men, 45 women.**

## Sources
Forbes (real-time billionaire profiles and the 2025-26 lists, such as America's Richest Self-Made Women) wherever Forbes publishes a figure: 27 rows. Celebrity Net Worth for everyone else: 80 rows. Second sources include Parade, Hello!, the Sunday Times Rich List (via ABC News Radio) and the Bloomberg Billionaires Index. Figures were collected on 28 October 2026.

## Notes
- **Forbes vs Celebrity Net Worth:** where both publish a figure, the video uses Forbes. The two can differ a lot: Celebrity Net Worth puts Spielberg at $11.5B, Oprah Winfrey at $5B and Tyler Perry at $850M (Forbes: $7.9B, $3.4B and $1.4B). Each gap is noted in its row.
- **Older estimates:** James Corden (May 2024) and Halle Berry (October 2024) rest on one older estimate. Eight women, among them Selena Gomez and Serena Williams, carry their figure from the Forbes 2025 list because Forbes has not published a newer one for them.
- **Re-checked on 9-10 November 2026:** the Forbes figures for Spielberg, Michael Jordan, Oprah Winfrey, Jay-Z, Taylor Swift, Kim Kardashian, Tyler Perry and Magic Johnson were unchanged. George Lucas had moved from $5.4B to $5.5B.
- **Joint figures:** the Sunday Times Rich List figure for Paul McCartney is joint with his wife. It is used only as a second source.
