# The World's Most Iconic Wedding Dresses: What They Cost

**Video:** [What $31 Million Buys You: The World's Most Iconic Wedding Dresses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Pv4lPEKtwk), published 6 August 2026
**Rows:** 74 cards, from Elizabeth Taylor (1950) to Zendaya (2026), in the order they appear in the video

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | Every card with its price type, confidence and sources. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Iconic_Wedding_Dresses.xlsx` | The same data colour-coded by confidence, plus a Stats sheet and a Method sheet |

## What's inside
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bride, Wedding Year, Dress Price on Card | As shown in the video |
| Price Type | **Reported estimate**: a figure published by the media. **Channel estimate**: no published figure exists, so the card shows our own rough estimate. **Not disclosed**: no price shown |
| Confidence | **Verified**: two published sources give the card figure. **Partial**: one source. **Unconfirmed**: sources conflict or no published figure was found. **n/a**: no price shown |
| Note | Designer and any disagreement between sources |

**Every price here is an estimate.** Brides and designers almost never disclose what a wedding dress cost. Media figures often disagree with each other, and some are adjusted for inflation.

## By the numbers
- **66** cards show a price and **8** say "Not publicly disclosed".
- Of the 66 prices, **40** are reported estimates, **25** are our own estimates and **1** is a whole-wedding cost (Angelababy, see Corrections).
- **Elizabeth Taylor** appears **8** times, once for each of her weddings.
- **Highest dress figure:** Queen Letizia of Spain (2004), $10.7 million, the figure Guinness World Records gives. **Lowest:** Bianca Jagger (1971), $100.
- **Median price** on a priced card, not counting Angelababy: **$25,000**.
- **5** cards show $1 million or more.

## Sources
Marie Claire, The Knot, Hello!, Nicki Swift, The List, The National, Women.com, GOBankingRates, Slice, Refinery29 and Guinness World Records, checked by Global Stat Tide in November 2026. Links are in every row.

## Corrections
- **Angelababy (2015):** the card shows $31,000,000 (printed as "$31000,000"). That is the reported cost of her **whole wedding**. The price of her Dior gown has never been published.
- **Sophie Turner (2019):** the card shows $850,000; our working file had $580,000. Neither figure has a published source. Both are our own estimates.
- **Elizabeth Taylor (1957):** the card shows $9,000, which is below the $10,000–$15,000 range in our working file. It is our own estimate.

## Notes
- **Elizabeth Taylor:** apart from the 1950 and 1991 gowns, none of her dress prices were ever reported. The card figures for the other six weddings are our own estimates.
- **Priyanka Chopra:** has two cards. The $24,000 card is her outfit for the Hindu ceremony (our estimate). The $2 million card is her Ralph Lauren gown (reported).
- **Grace Kelly and Coleen Rooney:** published figures range widely. See the Note column.
