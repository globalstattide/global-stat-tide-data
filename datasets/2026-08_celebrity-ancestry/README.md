# Celebrity Ancestry: The Heritage of 146 Famous Faces

**Video:** [The Real Ethnicity of 150 Famous Faces Is Stranger Than You Think](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJSnIZHbdHk), published 30 August 2026
**Rows:** 146 actors, singers and entertainers, from Charlize Theron to Slash, in the order they appear in the video

> **What "heritage" means here:** the countries or peoples that a person's parents, grandparents or earlier ancestors come from, **as the person has described it publicly or as published biographies document it**. It says nothing about citizenship or how anyone identifies. Every card was checked label by label against the person's biography.

## By the numbers
- **Most common heritages:** English 33 · German 30 · Irish 30 · Ashkenazi Jewish 22 · Italian 20 · Scottish 14 · French 12 · Spanish 11
- **Average of 2.39 heritages per card.** 47 people have a single heritage; 33 have four or more. Four cards show six: Gal Gadot, Jessica Alba, Joaquin Phoenix and Daniel Radcliffe.
- **85 of the 146 were born outside the United States**, 26 of them in the UK and 10 in Canada. Unexpected birthplaces include Keanu Reeves (Beirut, Lebanon), Bruce Willis (West Germany), Joaquin Phoenix (Puerto Rico), Isla Fisher (Muscat, Oman), Lupita Nyong'o (Mexico City) and Freddie Mercury (Zanzibar, today Tanzania).
- **Confidence:** 126 Verified, 18 Partial, 2 Unconfirmed.

## Sources
Each person's English Wikipedia biography (Early life and Personal life sections), which cite the person's own interviews, genealogy research and press profiles. Checked by Global Stat Tide, November 2026. Each row links to its source.

## Corrections
- **Jessica Alba:** the card includes **Spanish**. A genealogy study cited in her biography found that her surname was not inherited from a Spanish ancestor; her paternal line is Indigenous American. Her Mexican, Danish, Welsh, English and French heritage is confirmed. Marked Unconfirmed.
- **Olivia Wilde:** none of the four labels on the card (English, Scottish, Manx, German) is stated in her biography. Marked Unconfirmed.
- **Zoe Saldaña:** Dominican and Puerto Rican are confirmed; **Haitian and Lebanese** could not be confirmed.
- **Vin Diesel:** Scottish (mother's side) is confirmed. He never knew his biological father and describes himself as "of ambiguous ethnicity", so the other labels could not be confirmed.
- The other Partial rows have one or two labels that the source does not mention. The "What the sources say" column names them.

## Notes
- The video title says 150; the video shows **146** cards.
- Cards show at most six labels, so some documented ancestry is not on the card. For example, Daniel Radcliffe's father is from Northern Ireland and Naomi Watts's mother has Welsh heritage.
- **Ashkenazi Jewish** is treated as supported when the biography names Jewish ancestry from Central or Eastern Europe (for example Polish, Russian, Lithuanian or Hungarian Jewish).
