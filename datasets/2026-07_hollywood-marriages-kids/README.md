## By the numbers
- **Average:** 2.2 marriages and 2.9 children per star.
- **57** married exactly once; **21** married five times or more; **14** never married.
- **Most marriages:** Mickey Rooney (8). **Most children:** George Foreman (12).

## Method
- **Marriages:** legal marriages only. Remarrying the same person counts twice (Richard Burton, Robert Wagner). Annulled marriages count and are noted. Long-term partners do not count.
- **Children:** biological plus legally adopted children, including children who died young. **Stepchildren are not counted.**
- **Sources:** each row was checked against Wikipedia plus an independent source (People, Biography.com, Britannica, Variety, AP and others). Links are in every row.
