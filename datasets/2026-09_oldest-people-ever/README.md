# The Oldest People Ever: Validated Records vs Claimed Ages

**Video:** [OLDEST People ever From 110 to 1112](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yaszi7hp6g), published 20 September 2026
**Rows:** 106 people, from 110 to a claimed 1,112 years, in the order they appear in the video (youngest age first)

> **Only the first 63 rows are real, validated ages.** Everything after Jeanne Calment (122) is a **claimed age that no validation body has accepted**. The video says the same: "Everything you've seen up to this point is proven. Everything after this is not." Do not cite rows 64–106 as facts.

## Columns
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lifespan, Age, Gender, Country (as shown) | As shown in the video. Country is where the person lived; age is completed years |
| Status | **Validated**: age confirmed from documents by the Gerontology Research Group (GRG), LongeviQuest or similar bodies. **Unvalidated claim**: no documentary proof has been accepted |
| Born, Died, Exact Age | Full dates for the validated cases. "Living" means alive as of November 2026 |
| Born In (if different) | Country of birth (today's borders), where it differs from the country shown |

## By the numbers
- **Validated:** 63 people, 40 women and 23 men. Most common countries: United States 14, Japan 10, United Kingdom 5, Brazil 5, Spain 4.
- **Unvalidated claims:** 43, 28 men and 15 women. India 7 is the most common country.
- **The record:** Jeanne Calment, 122 years 164 days (France, died 1997). The highest claim in the video, 1,112 years, is more than nine times that.
- **Still living (November 2026):** Ethel Caterham (United Kingdom, 117), the world's oldest living person, and Yolanda Beltrão de Azevedo (Brazil, 115).
- **Born somewhere else:** 7 of the validated people lived in a different country from the one where they were born. Maria Branyas, shown for Spain, was born in San Francisco.

## Sources
Wikipedia's "List of the verified oldest people" and "List of the verified oldest men", which compile GRG and LongeviQuest validations, checked November 2026. Every validated row's age and years match those lists. Norman Spencer's dates come from LongeviQuest. Five people aged 110–111 who are not on those lists (Eileen Kramer, Eileen Ash, George Ives, Frederica Sagor Maas, Ruthie Tompson) were checked against their Wikipedia biographies.
