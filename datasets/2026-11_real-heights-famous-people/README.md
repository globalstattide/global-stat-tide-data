# The Real Heights of Famous People: 102 Legends, Shortest to Tallest

**Video:** [The Real Heights of Famous People Will Shock You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bnoMj8RtFOY), published 8 November 2026
**Rows:** 102 people from history, politics, film, music and sport, ranked from shortest (Simone Biles, 142 cm) to tallest (Shaquille O'Neal, 216 cm)

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data.csv` | The full ranking. Browse it right here on GitHub |
| `GST_Real_Heights_Famous_People.xlsx` | The same data, plus a Legend sheet explaining the confidence labels |

## Confidence labels
| Label | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified** | Two sources agree | 28 |
| **Unconfirmed** | One source, or the sources differ slightly | 61 |
| **Estimate** | A scholarly estimate (shown with ≈ in the video) | 5 |
| **Claimed** | A popular claim with no record, such as ancient figures (shown with ≈) | 8 |

Every row includes its sources, with short quotes in the Excel file. A **Fun Fact** column explains famous myths, such as why Napoleon was not actually short.

## Method
- Heights are adult heights in centimetres, rounded to the nearest centimetre.
- **Country** is the country each person is known by. Where the birth country is different, it appears in its own column.
- The **average** height in this list is about 174 cm.

## Corrections
- **Simón Bolívar:** the video card shows 168 cm (5'6"). The 2010 exhumation of his remains found a man of **1.65 m**, so the dataset uses 165 cm. Thanks to the viewer who flagged this.
